\begin{abstract}
\itshape
We prove unconditionally that the Hodge conjecture is equivalent to two statements about abelian varieties, that the locus on which either statement holds is dense in every family and is the whole family or a meagre set, and that the real multiplications by $\sqrt5$, $\sqrt6$ and $\sqrt7$ on K3 surfaces are algebraic along explicit families, and that the cubic real multiplication by $\QQ(\zeta_{9})^{+}$ is algebraic on the K3 surfaces of Mumford type attached to the quaternion algebra ramified at two real places. For each statement we build the object it needs next, a complex on a product of two Weil fourfolds and a class on an abelian fourteenfold, prove what it does, and prove at which step it stops. The two statements stay open.
\end{abstract}
