# Publication scope of v2.8.0

This release adds the 25 October 2026 dependency audit, the written repair
of F2 ⇒ AB (assuming F2), the precise F3′ correspondence argument, exact
Mumford representation calculations and three checked auxiliary Lean lemmas.

The pre-existing v2.7.0 manuscript PDF and TeX archive are retained as
historical material. They have not been rewritten or recertified in this
release. The correction notes in this package identify arguments and
wording in that manuscript that need revision, including the direction
of the sixfold invariant-dimension bound. Read the audit with the paper.

The full Hodge conjecture, global F2 and F3′ remain unproved. No algebraic
representatives for the two exceptional Mumford directions were constructed.
The split-model invariant vectors are not algebraic cycles. The Lean file
formalises subspace implications, not the complete geometric reduction.

Authorship remains Deep Bhattacharjee and Ushashi Bhattacharya. The new
audit and replacement argument require author review. Existing human-review
declarations for an earlier paper are not extended to the new material.

The archive is being published at the user's explicit request. Zenodo's
version DOI is recorded separately after its GitHub integration creates
the record. A DOI identifies an archive; it does not certify a proof.
