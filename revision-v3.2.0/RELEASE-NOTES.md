# Algebraic Correspondences and Hodge Classes on Abelian Varieties

Codimension-two Hodge classes on one Mumford fourfold times any power of the auxiliary sixfold are generated by divisor products at full-group fibres. The codimension-three quotient has dimension 3m^4. Its algebraicity reduces to the existing mixed-link problem. The universal conjecture and F2 and F3-prime remain open.
