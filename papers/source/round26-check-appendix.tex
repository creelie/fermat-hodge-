\section{The finite representation controls}\label{round26:app:carrier}
The small-representation argument uses the cyclic Galois orbit of each highest-weight triple. For total orbit dimension at most ten, the complete list before the real-form restriction is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{highest-weight orbit}&\text{dimension}\\\hline
(0,0,0)&1\\
(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(0,0,1)&6\\
(1,1,1)&8\\
(2,0,0),(0,2,0),(0,0,2)&9.
\end{array}
\]
The six-dimensional candidate has quaternionic type at a compact factor and requires multiplicity at least two. The nine-dimensional adjoint candidate cannot itself be a weight-one Hodge substructure. The remaining eight-dimensional representation has no adjoint constituent in any single exterior degree, as the full proof shows. The source archive includes \texttt{anc/check\_exact.py}, which enumerates this finite list and the degree-four weights.
